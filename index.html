About me

LEVI AI personal context

I was born in Viradouro, a small city in the countryside of São Paulo, Brazil. I got into IT in 2012 by working close to the hardware: running cable, configuring firewalls, looking after on-premises servers, and supporting Windows, Linux, and the network between them.

That hands-on start made me curious about how the whole system fit together, not only the piece directly in front of me. When something failed, I wanted to follow the problem all the way through: from the physical connection and operating system to the service and the person trying to use it.

I worked in traditional infrastructure at Linx, then moved deeper into automation and cloud at Magazine Luiza and Beblue. Hapvida brought architecture and internal advocacy into the mix. At Codeminer42, my work became explicitly SRE. Later, at Navisite and Lumenalta, I operated production platforms across cloud and hybrid environments for international teams.

Today I work as a Staff / Lead Platform Engineer at Arionkoder / iSeatz. I design and operate multi-account, multi-region AWS platforms on EKS and ECS Fargate, build reusable infrastructure with Terraform and CDK, and improve delivery, observability, and developer experience. I have worked fully remotely since 2019 with teams across Latin America, Europe, and Asia. I still like getting close enough to a problem to understand it, then automating the boring parts so the next person has less to fight with.
