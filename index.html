About me

LEVI AI personal context

I was born in Viradouro, a small city in the countryside of São Paulo, Brazil. I got into IT in 2012 by working close to the hardware: running cable, configuring firewalls, looking after on-premises servers, and supporting Windows, Linux, and the network between them.

That hands-on start made me curious about how the whole system fit together, not only the piece directly in front of me. When something failed, I wanted to follow the problem all the way through: from the physical connection and operating system to the service and the person trying to use it.

I began in traditional infrastructure, supporting Linux, Windows, FreeBSD, networks, firewalls, virtualization, web servers, and relational databases. As my work moved into automation and cloud, I started provisioning AWS and Azure environments with Terraform, Ansible, and Packer, building CI/CD pipelines, containerizing services, and writing Python and shell tooling to replace manual operations. That led into SRE, Kubernetes, GitOps, observability, incident response, and the daily work of keeping production systems reliable across cloud and hybrid environments for international teams.

Today I work as a Staff / Lead Platform Engineer. I design and operate multi-account, multi-region AWS platforms on EKS and ECS Fargate, build reusable infrastructure with Terraform, AWS CDK, and CloudFormation, and create delivery paths with GitHub Actions, GitLab CI, Azure Pipelines, and AWS CodePipeline. My work also covers OpenTelemetry, Prometheus, Grafana, CloudWatch, Datadog, SLOs, developer self-service, and the operational standards teams need to run services safely. I have worked fully remotely since 2019 with teams across Latin America, Europe, and Asia. I still like getting close enough to a problem to understand it, then automating the repetitive parts so the next person has less to fight with.
